# Hot-water safety

Use a stable, heat-safe vessel and avoid overfilling a kettle or pot. Keep
hands and face away from steam, never heat a sealed container, and keep an
electric kettle's base and cord dry.

Follow the appliance manufacturer's instructions. If heating water in a
microwave, use a microwave-safe cup and handle it carefully because apparently
calm water can be superheated. Keep children and pets away from hot vessels.
