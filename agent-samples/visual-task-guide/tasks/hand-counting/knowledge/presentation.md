# Presenting hands to the camera

Keep every fingertip and the wrist inside the camera frame. Face the fingers
toward the camera, separate the extended fingers, fold the remaining fingers,
and hold the pose steady long enough to be observed.

For numbers above five, place both hands side by side without overlapping them.
Even lighting and a background that contrasts with the hands make individual
fingers easier to count.
